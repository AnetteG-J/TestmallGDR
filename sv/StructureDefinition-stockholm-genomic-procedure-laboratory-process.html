Resursprofil: Stockholm Genomic ProcedureLaboratory Process

Officiell URL: https://pub.regionstockholm.se/fhir/ImplementationGuide/TestmallGDR/StructureDefinition/stockholm-genomic-procedure-laboratory-process Version: 0.1.0
Draft per 2026-11-06 Beräkningsbart namn: StockholmGenomicProcedureLaboratoryProcess

Represents the laboratory phase of a genomic case and ties together library preparation and nucleic acid sequencing for a specimen. A single genomic study can include multiple laboratory process instances.
