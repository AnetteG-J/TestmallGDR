Resursprofil: Stockholm Genomic Related Person

Officiell URL: https://pub.regionstockholm.se/fhir/ImplementationGuide/TestmallGDR/StructureDefinition/stockholm-genomic-related-person Version: 0.1.0
Draft per 2026-11-06 Beräkningsbart namn: StockholmGenomicRelatedPerson

The related person profile is used to represent the relationship between the proband and another biological relative. The profile is linked from Patient.link on the proband patient record, and the RelatedPerson resource itself points back to the patient and describes the relationship to the other person.

Användningar:

  • Denna Profil används inte av några profiler i denna implementationsguide
